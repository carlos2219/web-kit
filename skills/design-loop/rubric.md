# Rubric

Score each item from 1 to 5. A 4 means a client would not notice anything to fix. A 5 means it's as good as the reference.

| # | Item | A 5 looks like | Typical slop (≤ 2) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Hierarchy** | Within 3 seconds you know what the page is and what to do. One dominant element per view. | Everything is the same weight. Three equal cards. The CTA is lost. |
| 2 | **Typography** | Display type has tuned tracking and line-height, as specified in `DESIGN.md`. Line length is 45–75 characters. The rag is clean, with no orphans in headings. | System font. Default tracking. A heading wraps to one word on its own line. |
| 3 | **Spacing and rhythm** | A consistent vertical rhythm from the spacing scale. Generous whitespace that looks intentional. Things are aligned to a grid. | Uneven gaps. Cramped sections. Elements a few px off the grid. |
| 4 | **Color discipline** | Only tokens. The accent appears only where it converts. Contrast is ≥ 4.5:1 for text. | Gradient washes. Default Tailwind hues. Accent everywhere. Grey on grey. |
| 5 | **Personality** | A signature element (from Art direction or Components in `DESIGN.md`) that only this brand would have. | Could be any startup. Stock icons. A generic hero with a blob. |
| 6 | **Copy fit** | Copy names the pain, is specific, has one idea per section, and matches the tone rules in README. It fits the layout at every width and language. | Vague claims. English overflowing where Spanish fit. |
| 7 | **Mobile** | Designed for mobile, not merely squeezed to fit: the hierarchy is kept, tap targets are ≥ 44px, and there's no horizontal scroll. | Desktop layout stacked as-is. Tiny text. Overflow. |
| 8 | **Motion and detail** | Hover and focus states are visible. Entrances follow the Motion rules in `DESIGN.md`. `prefers-reduced-motion` is respected. Assets are sharp and don't shift the layout. | No focus ring. Janky or gratuitous animation. Layout shift. |
| 9 | **Craft vs reference** | Side by side with the reference, it holds up: the same level of finish in edges, radii, shadows, and icon consistency. | It's obviously the "AI version" of the reference. |
